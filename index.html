MDM Register

School record management for the PM POSHAN meal programme

About MDM Register

MDM Register is designed to help schools maintain records related to the PM POSHAN (mid-day meal) programme, including stock, distribution, and related settings and records.

Available features may vary by app version and configuration.

Records and Backups

App records may be stored on the device. When a user chooses the Google Drive backup feature, the app is intended to back up the records and settings included in that backup to the user's Google Drive.

The exact data included in a backup depends on the app's implemented functionality.

Privacy

For information about data handling and Google Drive backups, please read the Privacy Policy.
