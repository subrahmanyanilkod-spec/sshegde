MDM Register Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how the MDM Register app handles school records and settings and how the optional Google Drive backup feature is intended to work.

2. Information Stored in the App

Depending on the app version and features used, users may enter school-related stock, meal distribution, finance, and configuration records. These records may be stored locally on the user's device.

3. Google Drive Backup

If the user chooses Google Drive backup, the app is intended to upload the records and settings included in the backup to the user's Google Drive account. Google account sign-in and authorization may be required.

The exact data included in the backup and the Google Drive permissions requested depend on the app's actual implementation. Users should review the Google authorization screen before granting access.

4. External Servers

Based on the information provided by the app owner, MDM Register is not intended to send app records to a separate server operated by the app owner. Google Drive backup is a separate service selected by the user. This statement must be checked against the released app code and any third-party libraries before this policy is treated as final.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

This draft does not make a blanket claim that no data is ever shared. Before publication, the app owner should verify the app code, included software libraries, and Google Drive integration, then update this section to accurately describe any data transfers or sharing.

6. Data Security and User Choices

Users should protect their device and Google account. Access to files stored in Google Drive may depend on the user's Google account and Drive sharing settings. No specific security measure is promised here unless it is implemented and verified in the app.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local records can be affected by app data removal or uninstalling the app, depending on the app's storage implementation. Backup files stored in Google Drive are managed through the user's Google Drive account. Confirm the app's actual behavior before relying on these statements.

8. Children's Information

The app may be used to maintain school programme records. Do not enter unnecessary personal information about students. Before publication, the app owner should review the actual fields stored and add any required child-data disclosures that apply.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date at the top should be updated whenever the policy is revised.
